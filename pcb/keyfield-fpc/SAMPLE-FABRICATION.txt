GyreNano V27 — FIVE-PIECE ENGINEERING SAMPLE / 五片工程试样
This package specifies ED copper for prototype testing, not a qualified production design.
本包用于电解铜实物折弯、导通、焊接与装配试验；不是已验证的量产设计。

Quantity: 5 identical single blanks, each 97.176 x 96.472 mm.
2-layer PI FPC; nominal total flex thickness 0.11 mm.
Adhesiveless ELECTRODEPOSITED copper, 1/3 oz (nominal 12 um) per copper layer.
Yellow coverlay; ENIG 1 microinch; white silkscreen; no controlled impedance.
No factory stiffeners, no factory adhesive, no shielding film, no SMT, no edge gold fingers.
AOI and flying-probe testing; laser profile to Edge.Cuts, PTH and NPTH per separate drill files.
Other EDA: KiCad. Do not infer an FR4 stack from this design.
五片同款单片；双层PI，成品软板厚0.11mm，无胶电解铜，外层1/3oz，黄色覆盖膜，沉金1u英寸。
不做厂家补强、背胶、屏蔽膜、SMT或插拔金手指；白色字符，无阻抗要求。

CAM review: F_Mask/B_Mask specify coverlay openings, not solder paste.
Six shared FFC windows and the JP1 selector window are intentional; do not separate/remove them silently.
Only Edge.Cuts and drill files define factory machining; NO additional separation cuts.
The circuit has detachable regions with real R1_LINK/COL3_LINK copper connections.

不要增加工厂分割切线；任何线路、板框或开窗改动请先联系客户确认。
