# Integrated shells and FR4 retainers

Unchanged V14 upper shells and main retainers; original V8 thumb retainers. All files retain their historical assembly coordinates, in mm. Orient the STL in your slicer; the integrated STL files are not newly bed-oriented. Reuse the [V7 bottom trays](../3x5/).

These mounts/retainers are the existing 0.6 mm FR4 baseline. No 0.4 mm fit adaptation is included. [Static checks](checks/) are retained; no new geometry or slicing was performed during cleanup. The modeled M2 × 4 mm screw/head envelope is a trial assumption, not a qualified fastener selection. Fasten the right thumb retainer before arranging crossing ribbons.

For the bare-FPC route, keep these upper shells and replace all four retainers with [FPC supports](../fpc-supports/README.md).
