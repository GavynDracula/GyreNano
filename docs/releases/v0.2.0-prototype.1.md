# v0.2.0-prototype.1 — PCB sample snapshot

2026-11-13. This pre-release collects the current FR4/FPC experiments and integrated prints while sample boards are in transit. It does not claim completed physical qualification.

- Component-based PCB folders replace the chronological V1–V34 working tree.
- Four root folders: `files/`, `pcb/`, `docs/`, `development/`. Enclosure tools/reference inputs moved together, with public-file and upstream paths adjusted. Images and BOM/firmware notes are under `docs/`.
- V33 0.6 mm FR4, V32 isolated alternative and V27 FPC artwork/order ZIPs are preserved byte-for-byte.
- Corrected native EDA holder/sensor projects are the primary entry points. Older KiCad sources remain labeled references because their PTH mask openings differ.
- Integrated V14 shells/FR4 retainers and V31 FPC supports are indexed under `files/`; all geometry is preserved.
- Old generators and their cross-version inputs are frozen in the [engineering snapshot](https://github.com/GavynDracula/GyreNano/releases/download/v0.2.0-prototype.1/GyreNano-EngineeringSnapshot-20261113.tar.gz), with a [file manifest](engineering-snapshot-files.json). Historical report paths refer to this snapshot. No tool environments or authentication credentials are included.
- [Migration provenance](file-migration.json) records original paths/hashes; the holder footprint-table URI and documentation links are deliberately relocated. [Cleanup record](cleanup.json) records sizes and the snapshot checksum.

Pending: manufacturer acceptance where not yet reported, bare-board forming/continuity, FDM fit, hot-swap load support, complete FFC routing and powered/optical tests. 0.4 mm FR4 is not part of this release. No new slicing was performed.
